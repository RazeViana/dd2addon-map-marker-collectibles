# Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished logo image for a fantasy game mod's Nexus Mods page.
Primary request: create an original, polished logo for "Map Markers and Collectables by Raze".
Subject: a strong, simple compass-and-map-pin emblem with a small treasure sparkle, suggesting exploration and finding hidden treasures.
Style: refined fantasy cartography, engraved emblem, restrained antique metallic accents, crisp silhouette and elegant readable serif lettering. Professional identity design, not a gameplay scene.
Composition: landscape canvas, centered emblem above a balanced stacked wordmark, generous clear margins; strong readability even as a small thumbnail.
Backdrop: deep charcoal with extremely subtle map contour lines; lettering and emblem in warm parchment and antique gold.
Text, exactly: "MAP MARKERS" on the first title line, "AND COLLECTABLES" on the second title line, "by Raze" underneath. Spell COLLECTABLES with an A: C O L L E C T A B L E S.
Constraints: these are the only words. No official game logos, no borrowed mod branding, no screenshots, no interface elements, no extra slogans, no watermark, no mockup surroundings. Keep the wordmark prominent and legible. Produce one finished raster logo image.
