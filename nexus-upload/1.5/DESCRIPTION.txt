DARK ARISEN SUPPORT - TITLE UPDATE 4.0
Adds 140 Norgan Ducats and 389 Norgan chests with three new icon designs. Uncollected DLC markers and their artwork are enabled by default.

The DLC is optional. All base-game features work without it; expansion controls and markers activate only when Dark Arisen is owned and installed. Saved choices are preserved.

MAP MARKERS AND COLLECTABLES BY RAZE
Find Seeker's Tokens, Golden Trove Beetles and chests on your full map and minimap. Customize categories, artwork, colors and size; show collected items, use separate fog filters and track your first Seeker's Token. Progression filtering hides unavailable chests.

INSTALL
Requires a compatible REFramework: https://www.nexusmods.com/dragonsdogma2/mods/8
Close DD2 and install the complete v1.5 ZIP with Fluffy Mod Manager, or copy both reframework and natives beside DD2.exe. Keep your settings JSON. Open REFramework > Script Generated UI > Map Markers and Collectables by Raze.

COVERAGE
Fixed DLC placements only; random drops and appraised relics are outside coverage. The 185 Lost Rites chest positions and six unresolved Norgan rare chests stay guarded while unopened. Disabling Hide unobtainable chests previews them without confirming availability.

Unpublished test patch: DLC routing, pickup/hand-in, save reload and trial renewal still need live checks.
