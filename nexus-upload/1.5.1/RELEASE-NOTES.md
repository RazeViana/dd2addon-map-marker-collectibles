# Map Markers and Collectables 1.5.1: Spoils and Lost Rites

Adds 94 missing Spoils-related chest records at 86 distinct positions, plus three quest-linked Norgan chest records, directly from the installed Title Update 4.0 game files. Spoils Chests has a new default chest-and-gem icon.

The 185 potential Lost Rites chest positions now show by default and retain a separate potential-location control. Spoils follows Hide unobtainable DLC collectibles without a separate preview switch. The general DLC filter now defaults off so potential positions are visible; explicit saved choices are retained. The base-game filter remains independent and defaults on. All trial records have source-derived UI map IDs and bounds, keeping separate trials apart and their coordinates off outdoor maps. Full-map positions cover the selected trial across its floors; floor labels are not guessed.

A currently loaded, renewed closed trial/Spoils chest overrides its older opening in the save. Existing categories, saved artwork/visibility preferences, base-game progression, first-token guidance and the inaccessible Vernworth exclusion are retained. Dark Arisen remains optional; new DLC types are queried only after Steam confirms access.

Potential icons show where a chest can appear. Trial progress and rotating events determine whether it is ready to loot. Six Norgan rare chests and three quest-linked positions retain unresolved unlock guards when the general DLC filter is enabled. Random drops and appraisal contents are outside coverage.

The final v1.5.1 archive and Nexus upload materials are prepared for release. Automated runtime, data, routing, renewal, artwork and package checks pass. Native DLC rendering, pickup/hand-in, save reload and event/trial renewal have not yet been confirmed in-game. Preparing this package does not replace the published v1.5 Nexus file automatically.

Close DD2 and install the complete ZIP with Fluffy Mod Manager, or copy its reframework and natives folders beside DD2.exe. Preserve reframework/data/raze_MapMarkersAndCollectables_settings.json. Requires compatible REFramework. Source credits are included in docs/raze_MapMarkersAndCollectables/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
