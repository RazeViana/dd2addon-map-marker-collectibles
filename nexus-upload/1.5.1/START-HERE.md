- RELEASE-NOTES.md, CHANGELOG.md and NEXUS-DETAILS.txt describe the matching features.
- SHA256SUMS.txt and package-manifest.json verify the archive and payload.

The separate Nexus-Upload-Kit.zip is for keeping these upload materials together. Upload the mod archive inside it as the main file.
